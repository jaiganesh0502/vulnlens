WHY THESE SIGNALS EXIST

Defensive Telemetry Grounding

Vulnerability triage requires multiple independent telemetry signals. Each signal answers a fundamentally different defensive question:

The Three Questions Security Leaders Must Answer:

  1. Is the flaw technically dangerous? → Answered by CVSS
  2. Are adversaries actively exploiting it right now? → Answered by CISA KEV
  3. Will adversaries likely exploit it soon? → Answered by EPSS
  4. Can it hit our crown jewels? → Answered by Asset Exposure & Business Criticality

Real-World Defensive Case Study: Log4Shell

CVE-2021-44228
Vulnerability: Log4Shell (Apache Log4j)
CVSS Base Score: 10.0 (CRITICAL)
CISA KEV Listed: YES (Active in wild)
EPSS Probability: > 0.97 (Extreme)

Defensive Prioritisation Analysis

Log4Shell became a major defensive prioritisation event because of its technical severity and evidence of exploitation.

When Log4Shell was disclosed in December 2021, almost every enterprise discovered hundreds of vulnerable Java components across their IT estates. Patching every single instance simultaneously was humanly impossible.

Defense teams that succeeded applied contextual prioritization:

  • Hour 1-24: Patched internet-facing perimeter services (Identity, Portals, Gateways).
  • Day 2-3: Patched mission-critical internal databases and payment pipelines.
  • Week 2+: Remediated isolated internal developer sandboxes and offline reporting utilities.
